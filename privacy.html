Whirr — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

Whirr is an iOS client for Audiobookshelf, a self-hosted media server. This policy describes what Whirr does with your information.

The short version

Whirr collects no data about you. There is no Whirr server, no account with the developer, no analytics, no telemetry, and no third-party crash reporting or advertising code in the app. Nothing you do in Whirr is transmitted to the developer or to any third party.

There is no developer-operated service

Whirr does not have a backend. When you set up the app you enter the address of your own Audiobookshelf server. All of Whirr's network activity — signing in, browsing your library, streaming, downloading, artwork, and syncing your listening progress — goes to that server and nowhere else.

The developer has no access to that server, its contents, or any traffic between it and your device. Your relationship with that server is governed by whoever runs it, which in the ordinary case is you.

Diagnostics

Whirr uses Apple's MetricKit to record local performance information. That information is written to the device's own system log and is never uploaded — not to the developer, not to anyone. It is visible only to you, on your device.

Whirr writes no credentials, tokens, or authorization headers into any log, error message, or exported diagnostic file.

Tracking and advertising

Whirr does not track you. It contains no advertising identifier (IDFA), no App Tracking Transparency prompt, no advertising or attribution SDK, and no analytics SDK of any kind. No data is shared with data brokers or advertising networks, and no user profile is built for any purpose.

Links out of the app

An EPUB you read may contain external links. Whirr opens one only after you confirm it, only for http, https, mailto, and tel addresses, and only by handing it to the appropriate system app. Once a link opens elsewhere, that destination's own privacy policy applies. All other link types are refused.

Siri and App Shortcuts

Whirr's voice actions carry no title, author, account, server, or library information. They accept only a playback direction or a sleep-timer duration.

Children

Whirr is a client for a library you supply. It contains no first-party catalogue and shows no advertising. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Signing out of Whirr deletes your stored credentials, the local database for that account, all downloaded media, the artwork cache, and any exported diagnostics from the device. Deleting the app removes everything in its container.

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request, correct, export, or delete from the developer. Requests about the contents of your Audiobookshelf server should go to whoever operates that server.

Your rights

Whirr processes no personal data on the developer's behalf, so there is no controller-held data subject to access, rectification, erasure, portability, or objection requests under the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR, or the CCPA/CPRA. Whirr sells no personal information and shares none.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new "last updated" date. Material changes affecting how the app handles your information will also be noted in the app's release notes.
